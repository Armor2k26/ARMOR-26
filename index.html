Product Overview

What ARMOR actually does

ARMOR is a fully autonomous four-wheel Mecanum rover built to detect, map, and scrub airborne microplastics on industrial floors.

Application map

01

Patrol

2D LiDAR + ROS 2 Nav2 builds and navigates the live occupancy grid.

02

Capture

Intake blower pulls ambient air into a sealed darkroom chamber.

03

Classify

650nm laser illuminates particles; IMX500 classifies synthetic vs. organic on-chip.

04

Map

Positive detections are tagged with XY coordinates and pushed to the cloud.

05

Scrub

High-static HEPA blower activates at mapped hotspots to clear the airspace.

The competitive gap: static dust sensors can't tell safe organic dust (cotton) apart from toxic microplastics (polyester/nylon). ARMOR identifies the specific threat and actively removes it from the breathing zone.

Why ARMOR

Micro-vision. Macro-impact.

Open-loop purifiers just run blindly. ARMOR reads the environment, makes a classification decision, physically moves, and actuates a scrubber — based entirely on live data.

Micro

Fiber-level detection

A single airborne fiber crossing a 650nm laser beam is enough to trigger a classification. The Sony IMX500 tells a synthetic polymer's sharp, jagged scatter pattern apart from the round scatter of water or organic dust — on-chip, in milliseconds.

Macro

Plant-wide impact

Every detection becomes a heatmap point on the cloud dashboard — feeding occupational-hazard reduction in line with DGET guidance, and ESG/CBAM-ready audit data for the mill's compliance record.

Why the problem matters

Microplastics are no longer only an environmental concern.

Research has detected microplastic particles in human samples from multiple locations. The evidence is growing, while some headline estimates remain debated — exactly why ARMOR focuses on measuring and removing airborne exposure at the source.

Particles ingested / year

>90K

Estimated annual intake rises above 90,000 particles for primarily bottled-water consumption.

5 g/wk

A WWF-commissioned Newcastle model estimated roughly 5 grams per week; this is model-derived and contested.

17/22

Blood samples

Leslie et al. (2022) detected microplastics in 17 of 22 healthy adults.

Breast-milk samples

Ragusa et al. (2022) reported particles in 26 of 34 samples.

Evidence note: Microplastics have also been reported in lung tissue, placenta and arterial plaque. Brain-tissue findings reported in 2025 are preliminary and have received methodological criticism, so they should be treated as suggestive rather than settled science.

The closed loop — five operating states

Mapping

Rover navigates the facility, LiDAR sweeping the room at 10Hz.

Detection

Intake fan runs continuously. Laser diode is active. AI Camera runs inference at 30fps and IMX500 registers microplastic concentration above the safety threshold.

Remediation

Rover halts. HEPA scrubber activates at 100% duty cycle.

Scrubber Auto-Cycle

AI Camera monitors outgoing air quality. Once the area reads clean, the scrubber powers down and the rover returns to State 0.

The optics — laser scattering, explained

1

The chamber — a 3D-printed, light-proof tube: the darkroom.

2

The lighting — the 650nm laser diode shines perpendicular (90°) to the AI Camera's line of sight.

3

The physics — the camera stares into pitch black. When a fiber crosses the beam, it violently scatters the laser light.

4

The detection — a sudden, bright geometric flash appears against the black background. Synthetic fibers scatter light differently than round dust or water droplets.

5

The edge AI — IMX500 processes the shape internally, sending only metadata ("microplastic fiber, 92% confidence") to the Pi 4.

Major problems and solutions

Lens barrier

Issue

The AI Camera's fixed lens blocks raw wavefronts — no bare-sensor holography.

Fix

Pivoted to orthogonal darkfield scattering — the factory lens becomes a macro-gatherer instead.

CPU bottleneck

Issue

A Pi 4 crashes if forced to run SLAM and video inference at once.

Fix

IMX500 offloads 100% of image processing on-chip — the Pi 4 only handles movement.

Mecanum drift

Issue

Wheels slip on dusty floors, confusing internal odometry.

Fix

An EKF node fuses encoder + LiDAR scan-matching data to correct for physical slip.

Problems that will arise

Dust on the lens

Reality

Sucking plastic-filled air at a camera lens coats it in dust within an hour.

Fix

An "air curtain" of clean HEPA-filtered air blows across the lens to repel particles.

Driver thermal shutdown

Reality

A motor stall can spike current past 2A and burn out the TB6612FNG.

Fix

Aluminum heatsinks plus a software current-limit that cuts power on a detected stall.

LiDAR blind spots

Reality

The 2D scan plane misses small tools, dropped fabric, or anything below 15cm.

Fix

Phase 2 adds low-mounted ultrasonic sensors (HC-SR04) to the bumper.
